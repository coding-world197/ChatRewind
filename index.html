How to install

The app is not on the Play Store, so installing takes a few extra steps. It takes about five minutes.

  1. Download the APKUse the button above to download the file. It is about 25 MB.
  2. Open the file and tap InstallIf you see “Install blocked”, tap Settings and turn on Allow from this source for the app you opened it with (Chrome or Files). Then go back and tap Install.
  3. If Play Protect stops itOpen the Play Store, tap your profile photo, go to Play Protect > settings and turn off Scan apps with Play Protect. Turn it back on after installing. Google warns about this because the app asks for permission to read notifications.
  4. Open the app and finish the 3-step setupThe app guides you: permission to read messages, files access, and permission to run in the background. Each button opens the right Settings page. Switch it on and come back.
  5. You’re doneNew messages are now saved as they arrive. For statuses, view the status in WhatsApp first, then tap Refresh on the app’s Status tab.
If you see “App was denied access”: open phone Settings > Apps > ChatRewind, tap the three dots (⋮) at the top right, choose Allow restricted settings, then go back to the app and turn on notification access. Android 13 and newer show this after a fresh install.

Which permissions, and why

Notification access
To read message text from WhatsApp notifications. This is how deleted messages are saved.
All files access
To find WhatsApp’s status and voice files. Copies are made only from WhatsApp’s media folder.
Battery: unrestricted
So your phone does not shut the app down in the background and miss new messages.
Internet
Used only to check whether a new version is available. Your messages are never sent anywhere.

Know the limits

  • Only messages that triggered a notification are saved. If the chat is open, muted or locked, a message can be missed.
  • Statuses appear only if you have viewed them yourself.
  • Very long messages, photos and videos do not come through fully in notifications.
  • Android only. It is not possible on iPhone.
  • WhatsApp updates can change how things work. When that happens, the app gets an update.

Frequently asked questions

Is this like GB WhatsApp?

No. It is a separate app and does not modify WhatsApp. You keep using the official WhatsApp. Still, we are not connected to WhatsApp, and we cannot guarantee anything if its policies change.

Where does my data go?

Messages, statuses and voice messages stay on your phone. The internet is used only to check for updates. You can delete everything from the app’s Settings with one tap.

Why does Play Protect show a warning?

The app asks for notification access, and Google blocks such apps when they are installed from outside the Play Store. The install steps above explain how to get past it.

How do I get updates?

When a new version is out, the app shows a popup when you open it. Tap Download, then tap the file to install. Your existing data stays safe.

Will it show old messages?

No. Only messages that arrive after you install the app are saved.

Does it work on iPhone?

No. iPhone does not let third-party apps read notifications.
